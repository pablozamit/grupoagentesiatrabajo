# 80 hooks de storytelling (Chase Dimond)

Carrusel de **Chase Dimond** ([@chasehunterdimond](https://x.com/chasehunterdimond)) con **80 ganchos de storytelling para redes sociales**, organizados en diez tipos de historia con ocho plantillas cada uno. Las frases llevan huecos entre corchetes para rellenar con el caso propio. Compartido en el [post de @ecomchasedimond](https://x.com/ecomchasedimond/status/2122793870669062451) (17 nov 2026). Texto transcrito fielmente del carrusel, en su inglés original.

## The Transformation Story

*Use these when sharing your journey, progress, or personal growth.*

1. I went from [starting point] to [result]. Here's what changed.
2. A year ago, I was struggling with [problem]. Today, here's where I am.
3. Nobody talks about how difficult it is to go from [X] to [Y].
4. The biggest change between the old me and the new me wasn't what I expected.
5. I used to believe [old belief]. One experience changed my mind.
6. Here's what [number] months of working on [goal] taught me.
7. I didn't get [result] overnight. Here's what actually happened.
8. If I had to start my journey again, I'd do these 3 things differently.

## The Mistake Story

*Use these to share lessons from failures, wrong decisions, and expensive mistakes.*

1. The mistake that cost me [time/money/opportunity] was...
2. I thought I was doing everything right until this happened.
3. I wasted [time/money] learning this lesson the hard way.
4. One small decision created a much bigger problem.
5. Here's what I would never do again after [experience].
6. I ignored this warning sign, and it cost me.
7. My biggest professional mistake taught me more than my biggest win.
8. If you're about to [action], learn from the mistake I made first.

## The Turning Point Story

*Use these when something changed your thinking, career, business, or approach.*

1. Everything changed when I finally understood this.
2. I almost gave up on [goal] until one thing happened.
3. One conversation completely changed how I approach [topic].
4. I didn't expect one decision to make such a big difference.
5. The day I stopped doing [old habit], things started changing.
6. I was ready to quit until I discovered this.
7. One unexpected opportunity changed the direction of my career.
8. Here's the moment I realised I needed to do things differently.

## The Behind-the-Scenes Story

*Use these to reveal the work, decisions, and details people don't usually see.*

1. Here's what nobody sees behind [result].
2. What looked like a simple win actually took [time/effort] to achieve.
3. Let me show you what happened before the final result.
4. This is what building [business/project/career] really looks like.
5. The most important part of this project never made it to the final post.
6. Here's how we went from the first idea to the final result.
7. A look at what happened behind the scenes of [project].
8. The result looks simple. The process wasn't.

## The Contrarian Story

*Use these to challenge common advice with a real experience or clear reasoning.*

1. Everyone told me to [common advice]. I did the opposite.
2. I used to agree with [popular belief]. Not anymore.
3. The advice that sounds smart but doesn't always work is...
4. Here's why I stopped following [popular practice].
5. I know this might be unpopular, but [strong opinion].
6. What works for most people didn't work for me. Here's why.
7. The biggest lesson I learned was to stop doing what everyone recommends.
8. I wish more people questioned this common industry advice.

## The Relatable Story

*Use these to connect with an audience through everyday struggles and shared experiences.*

1. If you've ever felt like [specific struggle], I understand.
2. Nobody prepares you for this part of [career/life/business].
3. You know that feeling when [relatable situation]?
4. Sometimes the hardest part of [goal] is simply getting started again.
5. I know I'm not the only one who has struggled with this.
6. Here's something I wish someone had told me earlier.
7. Ever worked incredibly hard on something and still felt behind?
8. This is your reminder that [reassuring or useful lesson].

## The Client or Customer Story

*Use these to share real experiences, results, and lessons from working with others.*

1. A client came to us with [problem]. Here's what we discovered.
2. We almost made the wrong decision on this project.
3. Here's how one small change helped our client [specific result].
4. One customer question changed how we approached [product/service].
5. We learned more from this failed campaign than from our successful ones.
6. Here's what happened when we stopped doing [old approach] for a client.
7. A client once told me something I'll never forget.
8. The result was impressive, but the lesson behind it was even better.

## The Curiosity Story

*Use these to open a loop and make people want to discover what happened next.*

1. I noticed something unusual about [situation]. Here's what I found.
2. There's a reason I never [common action] anymore.
3. I didn't understand why this kept happening until I looked closer.
4. This one detail completely changed my perspective on [topic].
5. I was surprised by what happened after I tried [action].
6. Here's the part of [experience] that nobody expected.
7. Something didn't add up, so I decided to investigate.
8. The answer was hiding in the one place I hadn't looked.

## The Lesson Story

*Use these to turn personal experiences into useful advice.*

1. After [number] years of [experience], here's what I know for sure.
2. If I could give my younger self one piece of advice, it would be this.
3. I learned more from [experience] than I did from [expected source].
4. Three lessons from [experience] that I wish I'd learned sooner.
5. Here's the one principle that changed how I approach [topic].
6. I used to make this complicated. Now I follow one simple rule.
7. The best lesson I learned about [topic] came from an unexpected place.
8. Here's what [number] failed attempts taught me about getting it right.

## The Decision Story

*Use these to explain a difficult choice, a risk, or a moment when you had to choose differently.*

1. I had two choices: [option A] or [option B]. Here's what I chose.
2. Walking away from [opportunity/situation] was harder than I expected.
3. I said no to [opportunity] for one important reason.
4. This was the decision I kept postponing.
5. I took a risk that could have cost me [specific consequence].
6. Here's why I chose the harder path over the easier one.
7. I almost said yes, but one question made me reconsider.
8. Looking back, this was the decision that mattered most.
